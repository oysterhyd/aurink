period of Phase 0. They are kept as history and no longer change.

  `results.json`, images) and the card's report (`card-report.md`).

Current status and open items are in `docs/05-delivery-plan.md` §6.

Mark conclusions as [实测], [源码] or [待验证]. Nothing that has not been run is
"done" or "passed".
